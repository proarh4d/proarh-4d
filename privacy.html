03 Recipients and transfers

Data may be disclosed, strictly to the extent necessary, to:

  • providers of hosting, IT infrastructure and communication services (for example WhatsApp);
  • the payment processor of the online shop;
  • our accountant and, where applicable, specialist collaborators (surveyors, structural and building-services engineers, verifiers);
  • public authorities (town halls, the Romanian tax authority ANAF, institutions with approval and permitting duties), when the law requires it or for the files you have entrusted to us.

We do not sell personal data. If a provider is located outside the European Economic Area, the transfer takes place only with the appropriate safeguards provided by the GDPR.

04 Storage period

We keep data for as long as is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and for the period required by law:

  • tax and accounting documents — according to the statutory archiving periods;
  • project documentation and related correspondence — for the duration of the contract and of the warranty / professional liability period;
  • requests that did not lead to a contract — up to 12 months from the last interaction;
  • the Smart Connect account — for as long as the account is active or until deletion is requested.

05 Your rights

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • access the data processed and obtain a copy of it;
  • rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erasure (“the right to be forgotten”), where there is no legal obligation to keep the data;
  • restriction of processing;
  • data portability, in a structured format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

To exercise your rights, write to us at contact@proarh4d.com. We reply within 30 days at most. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP) — dataprotection.ro.

06 Security and changes

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect data: encrypted connection (HTTPS), restricted access to project files and secure digital storage.

We may update this policy to reflect changes in legislation or in our services. The version in force is the one published on this page, with the date of the last update shown above.
